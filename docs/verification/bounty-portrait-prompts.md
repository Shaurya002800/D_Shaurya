# Bounty Board portraits

Created with the built-in image generator, then resized to 576 pixels wide and encoded as WebP for the website. The existing loader preloads and decodes all four portraits before revealing the website. The personal portrait on the Experience poster remains the captain's own photograph.

The following shared prompt was followed by the corresponding subject prompt for each of the four generation calls.

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a portrait illustration printed inside a weathered pirate wanted poster on a portfolio website. A single centered head-and-shoulders portrait, front three-quarter view, recognizable One Piece anime character, expressive face, exquisite hand-inked manga linework, subdued natural colors, warm sepia printing on aged paper, slightly imperfect vintage lithographic ink, cinematic side lighting, simple muted parchment-and-sea background. Fill the portrait frame with the face and shoulders; keep entire head visible with modest breathing room. Portrait orientation. No border, no typography, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no glass panels, no weapons, no gore.

## Subject prompts

**Ace:** Portgas D. Ace from One Piece: young adult man, black tousled hair, freckles across his cheeks, warm confident smile, distinctive orange cowboy hat with two small blue badges, red bead necklace, dark open-collar shirt and orange vest.

**Garp:** Monkey D. Garp from One Piece: elderly broad-faced naval hero, short gray hair, gray beard and mustache, small scar near left eye, exuberant confident grin, white Marine officer coat draped over his shoulders, white shirt, dark tie, gold epaulets.

**Robin:** Nico Robin from One Piece: adult woman, long straight black hair, blue eyes, calm intelligent expression, sunglasses resting atop her head, elegant dark blue high-collared jacket, head and shoulders only, modest portrait.

**Rocks:** Rocks D. Xebec from One Piece: formidable adult pirate captain, wild voluminous swept-up black hair, strong angular face, broad confident toothy grin, dark high-collared pirate captain coat with worn gold trim, imposing charismatic head-and-shoulders portrait.
